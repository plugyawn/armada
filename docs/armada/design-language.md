# Armada design language

This is the design language the rebuilt frontend follows, reduced from the
design's specification (third final, 14 October 2026). Every value below is a
custom property in `web/src/armada/tokens/` or a constant read from it by
`web/src/armada/motion/`; nothing else may name a colour, a size, a duration or
a curve. The rules a stylesheet or a component is held to, with the lint or
check behind each, are in `.claude/rules/`.

This file is maintained by hand, from three documents that are not in the
repository: `var/armada-overhaul/design/final3/SPEC-DESIGN.md` (the design;
last changed at 03:02 on 14 October 2026; this file was last checked
against it later that morning), the user's brief
(`var/armada-overhaul/BRIEF.md`, through statement 21), and the reports of the
packages that built what is described here (`var/armada-overhaul/build/F3.md`,
`F4.md`). No script writes it. A change to the design or to the plan is made
in this file as well, by whoever makes it, and this file is what the code is
held to.

The product is Armada; its agent is Onix. People talk in bubbles; Onix's
words are ambient, on the plane with no bubble. People, Onix and chrome speak
in Söhne, with Hanken Grotesk as the open fallback behind it; what a machine
would accept is Geist Mono. There is no pixel typeface. Pixels are drawings:
Onix's sigil, which is its face, and the marks of what it is doing.

Paths that begin `proof/`, `shots/`, `review/` or `mock/` are in the design
folder, `var/armada-overhaul/design/final3/`, which is not in git; the stills in
`shots/` are what a built screen is compared with. A line marked **(P-n)**
follows a decision the user has not made yet.

## Principles

Ranked. A lower principle never buys anything at the cost of a higher one. Each has a test that returns a number.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Test, and the limit                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Truth.** Every mark is driven by a real event and is true now. What is normal is not announced. The checklist's caption counts steps that are done, and a finished run cannot be read as a running one.                                                                                                 | Words or glyphs for idle, done, clean, connected, "you": 0. For a live run, readable without scrolling: what it is doing, since when, how to stop it: 3 of 3.                                                              |
| 2   | **Agency.** The app never moves focus, scroll, panes or view by itself. Nothing reversible asks for confirmation.                                                                                                                                                                                         | Unrequested focus, scroll or pane changes in a walk: 0. Modal dialogs for something reversible: 0. Stop never sits where the default button is, and is not shown over the field while a run only waits for an answer.      |
| 3   | **Response.** What I asked for is whole and opaque in the frame of the input. What animates is travel, scale and what leaves. Only what I did not ask for fades in.                                                                                                                                       | Timers or animation callbacks between an input and its state: 0. Send task ≤ 8 ms at p95. First frame after each interaction shows the new state.                                                                          |
| 4   | **Stability.** Nothing the person did not act on moves, by one pixel, and one place is laid out as every other. The conversation is append-only.                                                                                                                                                          | Rectangles of header, conversation, column, band and field compared across a worktree, a channel, a direct message and Home, across the four views and the column's three contents: 0 px. Rows removed when a run ends: 0. |
| 5   | **Legibility.** No text under 12 px; two inks, each ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface text can touch; ring ≥ 3:1; a control's edge ≥ 3:1; no state by colour alone; hit areas ≥ 24 × 24.                                                                                                                           | Counted by the detectors and `tools/armada/palette.py`: 0 failures.                                                                                                                                                        |
| 6   | **Deference.** The conversation and the work are the screen. Chrome has no surface at rest. What stands beside the conversation is plain lists in the second ink, on the same plane: no box, no chip, no meter, no key and value.                                                                         | Planes: 2. Fills behind Onix's words: 0. Boxed sections, chips, meters or key-value rows in the column: 0.                                                                                                                 |
| 7   | **Once.** A fact is printed in one home; other places lead to it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Places that state a run is live: ≤ 3. The plan is in the column; the conversation keeps one line of record for a run that did work.                                                                                        |
| 8   | **Mac behaviour, Armada's face.** Every behaviour is the platform's; no pixel of chrome is borrowed.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | System faces in font stacks: 0. `backdrop-filter`: 0. Key caps at rest: 0. The twelve Mac conventions hold.                                                                                                                |
| 9   | **Type is the structure.** Hierarchy comes from size, weight and space, never from a box, capitals, tracking or a third grey. A verb that is a control is Strong; a selector and a label are Text.                                                                                                        | Sizes: 12, 14, 16, 20, 28 and no other. Characters below the reading size on a topic: ≤ 10%.                                                                                                                               |
| 10  | **Colour has four jobs and no fifth.** My words are my colour. A speaker's bubble carries a hint of their stone. Code has four roles and a diff two grounds. In chrome a hue means news: passed, needs you, failed. A fill never reports a state, with one exception: a bubble I reacted to keeps a wash. | Hues that mean news: 3, each ≥ 8 from the others and from my colour under normal, deutan and protan sight. Accent-filled controls in a view: ≤ 1.                                                                          |
| 11  | **One object.** A state change is the same element changing, moved by its cause, and it turns round from where it is.                                                                                                                                                                                     | The element created at send is the element that holds the answer. The typing bubble is the message. The default button is a small bubble of mine, and my bubble rises from it.                                             |
| 12  | **Derived, not chosen.** Unit 4, two lines (24 for what is said, 20 for chrome and code), control radius 6, one glyph weight on whole device pixels. Everything else follows.                                                                                                                             | Radii off {4, 6, 10, 14, 16, 18, 22, round}: 0. Rows of the conversation off the 4 px grid: 0. Literal colours, durations or easings outside the token file: 0.                                                            |
| 13  | **Chat first.** People talk in bubbles and Onix is fast. Every decision about the conversation is tested by one question: does it make the next message easier to send.                                                                                                                                   | Field ready in the frame of Return. First words painted within one frame of arriving. Words held back by an animation: 0.                                                                                                  |
| 14  | **Structure stays.** Repository, worktree, branch, commit, pull request, views, runs, threads and review are first-class and one action away from any topic.                                                                                                                                              | Header shows path, branch, pull request, latest commit and four views on every view. The checklist is readable at a glance from every view and scroll position.                                                            |

## Frame and layout

All in CSS px. Measured by `proof/measure3.mjs` (appendix B).

| Region | Bounds at 1440 × 900                                                                                             | Plane                     | Scrolls                          |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Nav    | x 0 to 248, full height; top row 52; account row pinned to the bottom                                            | Ground                    | its rows                         |
| Header | x 248 to the window's edge, y 0 to 88: line 1 is 52, line 2 is 36 with its controls on y 52 to 80, the nav's row | Content                   | never                            |
| Pane   | y 88 to 800. The conversation's scroller runs on to 836, under the band                                          | Content                   | natively, and only this          |
| Band   | y 800 to 836; its one line is 28 high at the top, 8 px clear of the field                                        | Content                   | never                            |
| Field  | x 272 to 1024, y 836 to 880: the gutter and the lane, 44 high; 20 below it                                       | Content, the field raised | never                            |
| Column | x 1064 to 1384, y 52 to 880; its first heading is on the header's line 2                                         | Content                   | the section that holds a history |

- **Grid.** The content plane is one grid: rows `88px minmax(0, 1fr) 36px auto`. Header, band and field are one DOM on every place and view; only the pane's child changes. The column is positioned over the grid and is not a track.
- **The composition.** The conversation and the column are one composition, centred in the pane and never less than 72 px from the nav. It is one continuous expression of the pane's width `W`, the same on every place:

  | Token                             | Value                                                                             |
  | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | `--col` (conversation)            | `min(704, W − 104)`; with the column, `clamp(704, 0.56 W, 840)`                   |
  | `--side-w`, `--side-gap`          | 0 and 0; with the column, `clamp(320, 0.265 W, 440)` and `clamp(40, 0.033 W, 56)` |
  | `--comp`                          | `col + gap + side`                                                                |
  | `--lx` (left of the conversation) | `max(72, floor((W − comp) / 2))`                                                  |

  The column stands when the window is 1408 or wider.

  | Window      | Conversation    | Column                 | Left of it, right of it | Empty share of the pane |
  | ----------- | --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
  | 1100 × 800  | x 322, 704 wide | folded into the header | 74, 74                  | 17.4%                   |
  | 1440 × 900  | x 320, 704 wide | x 1064, 320 wide       | 72, 56                  | 10.7%                   |
  | 1920 × 1080 | x 416, 840 wide | x 1311, 440 wide       | 168, 169                | 20.2%                   |

  _At 1920 the composition grows instead of its margins: it was 1143 px of 1672 in the second final and is 1335 now. It stops at 840 and 440 because a bubble is at most 640 wide and a list row with its trailing thing 440 px from its label stops being a row._

- **The lane.** Every shape of the conversation starts on the lane's left edge or ends on its right. Text inside a shape starts 16 px in: the text edge (x 336 at 1440). The path in the header, a view's word, the words in every bubble, Onix's words and the words in the field all start on it (measured: 336, five times). The 40 px before the lane is the gutter: a 28 px face, Onix's 28 px sigil and the field's Plus share one axis (x 294).
- **Two sides.** Everyone else's bubbles start at the left edge; mine end at the right. Onix's words and all blocks start at the left edge.
- **Measure.** Talk wraps at 608 px (a bubble is at most 640, and never more than 78% of the lane). A document reads on the same measure. A block spans the lane; code wider than that scrolls inside its block.
- **Two right edges.** The lane's (More; a document's switch; the field) and the composition's (line 1 of the header; the column; the switches of Files and Changes). The one thing narrower than the lane by rule is a link's preview, 368, because it belongs to the bubble above it.
- **Views and width.** Chat and README use the lane and keep the column. Files and Changes take the whole composition and the column folds into its heading, which does not move.
- **Below 1408.** No column. Its first heading is a 296 px row at the lane's right edge on line 2, one width whatever it holds, with More at its left, at one x on every place. It opens the column's lists as a raised sheet (344 wide, from the header's lower edge) on a press or ⌘J. The conversation does not move.
- **Narrow.** Down to 1100 the frame is as drawn. Narrower widths are not drawn; the rule carried from round 2 stands: below 1040 the nav becomes a sheet from the leading edge.
- **Electron.** `titleBarStyle: hiddenInset`. The system's window buttons take the first 72 px of the nav's top row. Header line 1 is a drag region outside its controls. No shape within 16 px of a window corner.
- **The scroll edge.** In the conversation, what runs on above the pane fades out under the header over 20 px (a mask on the pane, transparent at the header's edge to opaque 20 px below it), so no bubble, face or line of words is cut hard and no sliver of one shows at rest. The fade's depth follows the first 20 px scrolled; with nothing above it is 0. The conversation draws no line under the header. A document view (README, Files, Changes) keeps the 1 px line (`--line`) on the 80 ms fade while content lies above it. _Changed in the fourth prototype pass:_ it was a line and no mask everywhere, and in a still whatever the line crossed was simply not drawn; the still and the prototype now do the same thing.
- **What guarantees nothing moves.**
  1. One grid with fixed tracks, a column that is not in it, and one set of tokens for every place.
  2. Every value that changes sits where its width cannot push a neighbour: in a cell as wide as its longest form (the branch's distance, the commit's age, the checklist's caption, a view's count, the default button's verb), or anchored to a far end (the commit, the model).
  3. Things that appear fill slots that exist: the band for run state and for whoever speaks next, the slack beside a bubble for a reaction and a time, a nav row's trailing slot, raised surfaces for menus, the overlay and sheets.
  4. The conversation only appends. Onix's row is created in the send task, on the row after mine.
  5. A worktree with no run keeps its column, with one sentence where steps will be and the branch's commits where runs will be.

## Type

Two faces. `--f-human: "Söhne", "Hanken Grotesk", sans-serif` for people, Onix and chrome. `--f-code: "Geist Mono", monospace` for what a machine would accept. No system face is named anywhere. Emoji are content and fall through to the platform's emoji face.

Five sizes: 12, 14, 16, 20, 28. Two lines: what is said, and what is read as a document, is 16 on 24; chrome, lists and code are 14 on 20.

| Style          | Token         | Value                    | For                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Display        | `--t-display` | Halbfett 600, 28/32      | a document's h1; one to three emoji alone. Nothing in chrome                               |
| Title          | `--t-title`   | Halbfett 600, 20/28      | the place's one name; a document's h2                                                      |
| Heading        | `--t-heading` | Halbfett 600, 16/24      | a heading in an answer; a document's h3                                                    |
| Talk           | `--t-talk`    | Buch 400, 16/24          | what is said: a bubble, Onix's words, the field; a document's prose; a Home row's sentence |
| Talk, stressed |               | Kräftig 500, 16/24       | stress and a mention inside talk                                                           |
| Strong         | `--t-strong`  | Kräftig 500, 14/20       | a verb that is a control; a list's heading; a repository in the nav                        |
| Text           | `--t-text`    | Buch 400, 14/20          | every label, row and fact in chrome; a view's and a segment's name                         |
| Label          | `--t-label`   | Kräftig 500, 12/16       | a name over a turn, a day, New, initials in a face                                         |
| Caption        | `--t-caption` | Buch 400, 12/16, tabular | times, durations, counts, receipts                                                         |
| Code           | `--t-code`    | Geist Mono 400, 14/20    | code, paths, shas, branch names, commands                                                  |
| Line number    | `--t-code-s`  | Geist Mono 400, 12/20    | the numbers in a source or a diff gutter                                                   |

- _Why 16 for talk._ A messenger sets what people say larger than its chrome; at 14 in a 36 px bubble the room read as a work log. 16 on 24 with a 6 px inset keeps the 36 px bubble. The size is one step from chrome's (1.14), so the conversation is the largest text on its plane after the Title.
- _Why 14 for code._ Geist Mono's advance at 14 is 8.4 px: 80 columns are 672, and with a block's two 16 px insets that is 704, the lane. The lane's width is derived from it. One size for code everywhere removes the 13.33 the second final carried.
- Inline code sets no line box of its own (`line-height: 0`): a line that holds code is 24 or 20 high, never 21. It has no fill behind it.
- A verb that is a control is Strong. A selector (a view, a segment of a switch) and a label are Text: choosing is not doing.
- A row's weight never changes with selection, hover or unread. Unread is ink and a trailing mark.
- Numerals that change, or that stand in a column, are tabular and sit in fixed cells: the band's clock, a count in the nav, a reaction's count, durations, a table's figures. The test font has no tabular figures; the mocks build the cells (`fig()` in `kit.js`), which is also how a changing figure keeps its place in the product.
- A mention is Kräftig. A mention of me is Kräftig in my colour, with no fill. A link is underlined in my colour.
- Truncation has one spelling: a path or a command that does not fit is cut in its middle at one ellipsis with no space beside it; running text ends in one ellipsis.
- No capitals as labels, no tracking, no word-spacing.
- Söhne here is Klim's test file (A to Z, a to z, 0 to 9, full stop, comma, hyphen). Every other glyph in the shots is Hanken Grotesk at 106%. The licensed files drop in with no code change.

## Colour and surfaces

The colour block of `web/src/armada/tokens/tokens.css` is written by `tools/armada/palette.py`, which derives every value in OKLCH and refuses to write a palette that fails a gate. A palette is one file of colour tokens; loading `palette-tide.css`, `palette-citrine.css` or `palette-onyx.css` after `web/src/armada/tokens/tokens.css` is the whole of a swap (`?p=tide`).

**Rules.**

- Two planes: ground and content. Content is not an inset card. A raised surface is opaque and carries a 1 px edge, so its shape holds where a shadow cannot.
- Shadows: `--shadow-raised` under what is transient. `--shadow-field` under the field, which reaches 14 px into the 20 px below it; in dark the field has its edge and a 1 px lighter top line and no shadow.
- A fill is one of: hover, pressed, selection, a block, a bubble, my bubble, the default button, a reaction. A fill never reports a state. Two exceptions: an unsent bubble is half its colour until it is sent, and a bubble I reacted to keeps a wash of my colour from where the chip sits.
- In chrome a hue is one of three and means one thing. Needs-you also colours its two words in the band. Failed colours its footer line. Passed colours the mark of a result I waited for (checks passed). Nothing normal is coloured: live, present and done are ink.
- Code is coloured on its own surface: four roles, the same in both themes and in every palette. Red and green are left to the diff. Counts of added and removed lines take the diff's two inks wherever they are printed.
- Tide (sea-grey stone, one petrol; everyone else one sea-grey) and Citrine (slate stone, one yellow with ink on it; needs-you blue) pass the same gates. Onyx is the second final's graphite on this round's roles, kept for comparison. In every palette needs-you is a hue of its own, never the accent.

## Icons and marks

Two drawing systems with two meanings, and nothing borrowed from one by the other.

**Glyphs: strokes are for what people use.** 49 glyphs in eight groups. A 16 px box with a 14 px live area. One weight on whole device pixels: 1 px at 1x, 1.5 px nudged a quarter pixel at 2x. Butt caps, round joins, `currentColor`; the only fills are whole-pixel squares. One glyph a concept, never scaled. The sheet is rendered at 1x and 2x in both themes (`shots/s5-kit-*.png`). Redrawn this pass: `steer` (a straight shaft and one square bend, whole pixels at 1x) and `sound` (one arc). Known and not fixed: the diagonals of `clip`, `link`, `edit` and `expand` are lighter than the set at 1x.

**The sigil is Onix, and nothing else.** A cut stone with an unbroken outline, one broad bed and one vein inside it, as onyx is banded. An original mark; it depicts no character. Its cell is 2 px at every size it is shown, so it is one pixel object: 10 cells at 20 px, 14 at 28, 32 at 64. It is first ink and it never loops.

- 28 in the gutter of each of its turns, where a person shows a face, and beside a Home row it is the actor of.
- 20 in People, and in the field, where it is whole when Onix will answer and an outline (`20h`) when the message is for people only.
- 64 on an empty worktree and nowhere else.
- It is not a state mark and not an author's stamp: the band begins with a mark, and a commit Onix made carries its name.

**Marks: pixels for what Onix is doing.** Seven run and step marks on a 7 by 7 grid of 2 px cells, told apart by shape alone: done (a tick with one bend), live (a stone), waiting (an empty box), needs you (a diamond), failed (a cross), paused (two bars), stopped (one bar). Colour only on needs you and failed, and on a passed check. 14 px; 28 on Home's rows that need me.

**The working mark: the one loop.** The live stone with one bed stepping down through it, four steps of 200 ms, as one sprite moved in steps, so no frame is blank. It is the head of the stream: it stands where the next word will be and takes no room. A live run has this loop and no other. A person's typing mark is the opposite system on purpose: three round dots that grow and darken in turn.

## Motion

Everything moves. Frequency and distance from the person's attention set amplitude, duration and contrast, never presence. Only `transform` and `opacity` animate, and colour on the fades. `web/src/armada/motion/` is the only file that calls `element.animate`; the stylesheet half is the hover and press layer, the header's line and the working mark. The motion module and the prototype are their engineer's; this section is the design they are built to. Lines marked _new_ answer the interaction critique and the user's advocate and are specified here, not yet verified by the design lead in the prototype.

**Tokens.**

| Token    | Response | Damping | CSS duration | Overshoot | For                                                                                                 |
| -------- | -------- | ------- | ------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `touch`  | 0.15     | 1.0     | 210 ms       | none      | in place under the pointer or caret: a fill settling, a figure, a pane settling, the feed advancing |
| `move`   | 0.25     | 1.0     | 340 ms       | none      | what travels or opens because I acted: my bubble, a thumb, a popover, a sheet, the nav's block      |
| `settle` | 0.32     | 1.0     | 440 ms       | none      | what arrives without my doing it                                                                    |
| `land`   | 0.30     | 0.72    | 360 ms       | 3.8%      | textless objects that arrive: the working mark, a reaction's glyph, a tick                          |

Fades: in 80 ms, out 60 ms, and `trail` 60 ms for a hover fill leaving (it was 120: one row lit behind the pointer, not two). `step` 100 ms. `burst` 280 ms, `shake` 200 ms. `pending` 400 ms, `quiet-soft` 10 s and `quiet` 40 s are thresholds. Amplitudes: `rise` 8 px, `pane-rise` 4 px, `dip` 0.96, `from` 0.7, `follow` 20 px. No bézier, no fifth spring, nothing over 440 ms.

**Limits.**

1. What I asked for is whole and opaque in the frame of the input. No timer, `await` or animation callback stands between an input and its state.
2. What I did not ask for fades in over 80 ms.
3. Layout is written once; the difference is released with a transform.
4. Every animation yields to the next input from its current value and velocity.
5. One loop on a live run, the working mark, in the pane being read; and a typing person's dots. The sigil and the band's mark are still.
6. Only a textless object may overshoot. Text never scales. _New:_ a surface that holds words (the picker, the palette, a sheet) settles 4 px like a pane and does not grow from 0.96.
7. Reduced motion collapses the four spring durations to 1 ms, keeps the fades and holds loops on one frame. There is no blanket rule. _New:_ a refusal with motion reduced shows on the field's edge in the failure ink for 200 ms.

**Choreography that is fixed.**

- **Hover.** The fill is a layer behind the label: 30% in the frame of entry, whole within 80 ms, settling from a slightly smaller box on `touch`; it leaves over 60 ms.
- **Press.** `--fill-press` in the frame (the deepest fill, as the tokens table above has it), arriving at 0.96 of the box and settling on `touch`. Selected is Fill-3. Views and places answer on the press, with the primary button only, and on Return and Space from the keyboard.
- **Focus.** One ring for the window: 1.5 px in the accent, 2 px off the control on its radius. It follows its control through scroll and resize.
- **Selection.** One thumb a list. It travels on `touch` under the keys and on `move` under a press.
- **Typing.** The glyph in the frame. The default button takes my colour on the 80 ms fade when the field first holds words. The field grows a line at a time to eight lines and then scrolls inside its shape; the feed's shift is released on `touch`.
- **Send.** In the keydown task: my bubble exists in its final form, the field is empty and keeps focus, Onix's row exists with its mark, the sound is scheduled. _New:_ my bubble is born where the default button stands, which is a small bubble of mine, and rises to its row on `move`, under 80 px, with its time riding beside it; the feed advances a row on `touch`.
- **Onix, fast.** The mark stands where the first word will be. Words are appended as they arrive, whole. _New:_ a chunk may settle from 60 percent ink over the 80 ms fade; it is never held back and never reflows what is above.
- **The band and a fast answer.** _New:_ the band, the nav row's mark and the verb Steer wait on silence or structure, not on age: they appear only if no word has arrived after `pending`, or the run has a plan or a tool call, or the stream has run 2 s. A one-second answer changes nothing but the words.
- **A run that lasts.** The band reads `Working 0:01` with Stop; the word and its clock are one group with the clock 8 px after the word. The checklist's steps appear in the column on the fade. A step completing: its mark stays until the tick replaces it, on `land`; the caption's figure rolls. The clock counts seconds for ten minutes, then minutes.
- **A run ending.** A run ends one way (done, stopped, failed) and every place that shows it is drawn from that one state in the same frame: the turn's footer, the checklist's step and caption, the row in Runs, the checks in Changes, the nav's mark, the band and the verb. The mark leaves on the 60 ms fade. The band empties on the fade. A footer line stands under the turn if it changed files, failed or was stopped. **The room under a live turn:** from the write that bears it, a turn that is being written holds under its last line the room its footer will take (a 24 px line and its 4 px gap); the footer lands in that room, so Stop, a failure and the run's own end move nothing, at any moment of the run, a run stopped inside its first line included. A turn that ends with no footer keeps the room until the next write to the conversation, which takes it back inside its own motion. Stopped: the step that was live takes the stopped mark, its line reads `Stopped by <name>`, the caption reads `Stopped 11:39`. Failed: the failed mark, `Failed after 40s` with Retry, and the caption keeps its count.
- **A run that asks.** The band reads `Onix asks` and how long, in the needs-you colour, with no Stop. The options are buttons; 1 to 9 answer while the field is empty.
- **A person typing.** Their face and a bubble of their stone with three dots appear in the band's slot on the fade. When their message arrives that element becomes the bubble.
- **My reaction.** Made from the overlay or a key, never a double-click. The chip lands on `land` from 0.7, seated on the bubble's outer edge; three 8 px copies of the glyph leave it and fade within 280 ms; the bubble is knocked 3 px and returns on `touch`; a wash of my colour spreads from the chip's seat and stays. Pressing a chip of mine takes it back: the wash and the chip leave on the fade. Someone else's reaction fades in and moves nothing: on a bubble that shows its time, a chip that is new stands after the time (the time keeps its place), and joins the others the next time the conversation is drawn; a count that changes rolls in its cell. No row changes height.
- **The overlay.** _New:_ it appears after the pointer has rested 120 ms on a message, on the 80 ms fade, and travels to the next message on `touch` while it is up; it leaves on the fade.
- **Model and effort.** The popover opens upward from the model, on the band's line. A press on a model or an effort commits at once; Esc alone reverts.
- **⌘ held.** _New:_ the default button reads Queue only after ⌘ has been held alone for 150 ms with words in the field; any other key, blur or a hidden window puts it back.
- **Views, topic switch, nav, the column, a count, refusal.** As in the second final: the new pane whole in the frame and settling 4 px; a topic switch keeps the view; the nav's block travels as one opaque piece; a thread or a trace is whole in the column in the frame; a figure rolls 8 px; an empty send shakes the field ± 3 px.

**Never animates:** text size; a layout property; the frame; a bystander's position; scroll under the hand; a control's hit rectangle; a bubble's fill by state.

## Sound

Unchanged in this pass, and not the design lead's files. On by default in the product, in a set that never sounds for navigation or for what other people do in the room being looked at. One control mutes everything (Sound, in the workspace menu). Material: one small bar of hardwood struck with felt for people's events; the same pitches gated on and off for Onix. D major pentatonic.
